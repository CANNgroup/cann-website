Postdoctoral Researcher

James McAllister

Recurrent neural networks, neural dynamics and the mechanisms of sequence working memory and planning across species.

About

I am broadly interested in recurrent neural networks and neural dynamics. I study their theoretical properties as well as their use as models of neural circuits and computation in the brain.

In my current postdoctoral research, I am studying how recurrent networks in the prefrontal cortex across different species support sequence working memory and planning, with a particular focus on the neural mechanisms underlying hierarchical and abstract cognition. This research asks how changes in the prefrontal cortex might enable higher and more complex cognitive capacities.

Methods & interests

Recurrent neural networks, neural dynamics, theoretical modelling and comparative neuroscience.
