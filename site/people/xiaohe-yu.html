Xiaohe Yu

About

I am Xiaohe Yu. I have a background in applied mathematics and complex systems, and I am currently a PhD student in the CANN lab.

My research investigates how psychological stress affects cognitive computation within large-scale brain networks.

Research

I aim to understand how acute stressors, such as uncertainty, trigger catecholamine release in the brain, and how elevated catecholamines in turn modulate neural circuits, brain regions and behaviour.

Background

Applied mathematics and complex systems.

Research interests

Psychological stress, uncertainty, catecholamines and cognitive computation in large-scale brain networks.
