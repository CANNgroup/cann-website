Postdoctoral Researcher

Yufan Wang

Cross-species cortical alignment through neuroimaging, transcriptomics and computational approaches.

About

Yufan is a postdoctoral researcher at the Oxford Centre for Integrative Neuroimaging. His research combines neuroimaging, transcriptomics and computational approaches to investigate shared and species-specific patterns of brain organisation.

He aims to develop a unified framework for cross-species cortical alignment, enabling the translation of findings between animal models and humans.

Methods & interests

Neuroimaging, transcriptomics, computational approaches and cross-species cortical alignment.
