Join CANN

Come work on the link between anatomy, neural dynamics and cognition.

Most people joining the lab have experience with both neuroscience and computational modelling. You do not need to have followed one particular path, but applications are strongest when you can show serious engagement with both.

If you’re from a low socioeconomic background, or another community underrepresented in our field, we’d love to hear from you.

Before contacting Seán
Please check the funded routes below first.

PhD students and postdocs usually enter CANN through funded university schemes, fellowships or advertised posts. If you identify a route that fits, then get in touch to discuss scientific fit and supervision.

Before a PhD

Paid RAs & degree projects

There are too few funded pre-PhD research positions. To partially mitigate this, Seán aims to support at least one paid pre-PhD Research Assistant whenever funding allows.

We can also sometimes supervise research that is a formal part of an undergraduate or postgraduate degree, subject to capacity and the rules of the degree programme.

Our policy

Research deserves to be paid.

Unless the research is a formal part of a degree, we do not take on people to do unfunded research. Unpaid research is a bad influence on our community because it:

  1. favours people who can afford to work for free;
  2. puts undue pressure on people who need another job to support themselves; and
  3. contributes to talented scientists leaving research because they cannot afford to work unpaid.

We recognise that paid pre-PhD opportunities are still far too scarce. Supporting paid RA positions where we can is one practical way we try to reduce that barrier.

Found a funded route that fits? Tell us what you want to work on.

Please send a CV, a short description of the research questions that interest you, and the funding route or advertised position you are considering.
